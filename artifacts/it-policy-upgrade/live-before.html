Section 01

Computer User Credentials

Creation of Active Domain Account for users at Active Directory Server.

1. Employees using company computer is required to have their own user account and created with user’s profile information and photo.

Active Domain Account Process

a. New users must fill-out an account requisition form with approved by their department head. Forms are available at IT department.

b. IT admin will register all new accounts on Active Domain Server.

  • Providing USERNAME and temporary password. (Password must not contain user’s name; minimum of 7 characters with combination of alphanumeric and special characters.

4. Install Standard Software and should be performed by IT Administrator only.

5. Accessing Yakult official website.

Section 02

Desktop / Laptop / Printer Request

To ensure all IT equipment and consumable requests are properly documented, checked, approved, and processed.

Step-by-step procedure

  1. Prepare the request. The requesting department completes its requisition form and prepares a request letter stating the equipment needed and its purpose.

  2. Obtain department approval. The requester secures the signature of their Department Head or Manager on the request letter before submitting the documents to IT.

  3. Submit to IT. The requester forwards the signed request letter and requisition form to the IT Department for checking. IT may clarify the specifications or intended use.

  4. Prepare IT requisition. After checking the request, the IT Secretary prepares the official requisition form and attaches the supporting documents.

  5. Secure management approval. The requisition is submitted for approval by both Sir Rod and Sir Kawasaki. Requests requiring correction are returned for completion.

  6. Forward to Materials. Once approved, IT forwards the complete documents to the Materials Department for Purchase Request (PR) processing. The IT request endorsement is then complete.

Important reminders

Incomplete forms or missing signatures must be corrected before processing. Forwarding an approved equipment request to Materials completes IT endorsement, but does not mean the item has already been purchased or delivered.

Section 03

Ink / Toner Request

To ensure all IT equipment and consumable requests are properly documented, checked, approved, and processed.

Step-by-step procedure

  1. Submit request form. The requester fills out an ink/toner request form, indicating the printer or toner type and the quantity needed.

  2. IT checks and signs. IT staff verifies the requested item and availability, assists the requester if needed, and signs the form.

  3. Requester signs. The requester signs the form to acknowledge the request and the item to be issued.

  4. Release and record. IT releases the correct ink/toner and records the issuance for inventory and tracking purposes.

Section 04

Employee Internet Access Request

This procedure applies to employees who need internet access for work. It covers request-letter approval, IT review and computer connectivity verification.

Step-by-step procedure

  1. Employee prepares a request letter. The requester states their name, department, computer or workstation details (if known), and the work-related reason internet access is needed.

  2. Department Head / Manager approval. The employee submits the letter to their Department Head or Manager for review and signature.

  3. Sir Rod reviews and signs. The signed request letter is forwarded to Sir Rod. Processing proceeds only after his approval/signature.

  4. Submit approved request to IT Admin. The requester or department forwards the fully approved letter to the IT Admin for coordination with the technical team.

  5. IT Technical Staff checks the computer. IT verifies the workstation and existing internet connection, performs any required network configuration, and tests access.

  6. Confirm completion. IT confirms the internet connection works and informs the employee that the request is completed.

Important reminder

A request is not considered approved until the required signatures are complete. If access cannot be established, IT will inform the requester and follow up on the technical issue.

Internet Access Process

Process paths

  • Start → Provide letter of request for Internet/Website/E-mail/others → Approved by Department Head → Approved by Comptroller → Forwarded to IT Department → End.

Section 05

Guest Internet Access Request

This procedure is for guests or visitors requiring temporary internet access through the Shirota - Guest Wi-Fi network. Access is subject to approval by an authorized sponsor.

Step-by-step procedure

  1. Connect to Shirota - Guest. Open the device Wi-Fi settings and select Shirota - Guest. A sign-in or registration portal should appear.

  2. Open the guest portal and select Register. At the Yakult Guest Wi-Fi login page, select Register to request new access. Do not use the sign-in fields unless an account has already been issued.

  3. Enter the registration details. On the registration screen, provide the guest email address and the email address of an authorized sponsor who can approve guest internet access.

  4. Submit the registration. Check that both email addresses are correct, then select Register. The sponsor must be the person authorized to approve the guest request.

  5. Wait for sponsor approval. The portal displays Waiting for approval. Internet access is not yet authorized. The guest should wait for the nominated sponsor to review the request.

  6. Continue after approval. Once the screen displays Request approved, select Continue to proceed with connecting to the guest network.

  7. Confirm the internet connection. Return to Wi-Fi settings and confirm that Shirota - Guest appears as connected. Open a permitted website to verify that internet access works.

Section 06

IT Software Installation and Access Request Procedure

To establish a clear approval process for employee requests to install software or applications or access online services for work-related purposes.

Scope

Applies to employees requesting applications such as Viber and Zoom, or access to services such as ChatGPT, Gmail, YouTube, Outlook, Webex, Microsoft 365 and MS Teams.

Step-by-step procedure

  1. Prepare the request letter. The requesting employee prepares a request letter indicating their name, department, requested software/application or online service, and the specific business purpose or reason for the request.

  2. Obtain Department Head / Manager approval. The employee submits the letter to their Department Head or Manager for review. The Department Head or Manager signs the letter if the request is justified.

  3. Forward directly to Sir Kawasaki. The department forwards the signed request letter directly to Sir Kawasaki for evaluation and approval. The letter does not go to IT before this review.

  4. Await Sir Kawasaki’s decision. Sir Kawasaki reviews the request and either approves or rejects it. If rejected, the process ends and the requester is informed. Only approved letters proceed.

  5. Forward approved letter to IT. After Sir Kawasaki approves the request, the signed letter is forwarded to the IT team for implementation.

  6. Install software or configure access. An IT technical staff member checks the approved request, installs the software/application or configures the authorized access, and verifies that it works properly.

  7. Complete the request. IT informs the requesting employee once installation or access setup is completed. The approved letter may be retained for reference and documentation.

Important reminders

  • Use a request letter, not a request form.

  • State the employee, department, software/service, and business purpose clearly.

  • Department Head / Manager approval and Sir Kawasaki’s approval are required before IT proceeds.

  • No installation or access setup is performed for rejected requests.

Section 07

IT Hardware and Software Support Request Procedure

To provide a consistent process for receiving, responding to, resolving, and documenting employee requests for IT hardware and software technical support.

Scope

Applies to employees needing assistance with company computers, laptops, printers, peripherals, approved software, and related technical issues. New equipment purchases, new software installations, and access approvals must follow their separate request procedures when applicable.

Ways to submit a support request

  • Phone Call — Currently used. Employee contacts the IT Department by telephone and explains the concern.

  • Online Messaging — Currently used. Employee sends a message through Messenger, Viber, or Webex with the issue details.

  • Employee Portal Ticket — Pending implementation. Employee submits a support ticket through the company employee portal.

Step-by-step procedure

  1. Report the issue. The employee contacts IT through a phone call or approved messaging platform, providing their name, department, device or software involved, and a description of the problem.

  2. Receive and record the request. IT acknowledges the concern and records essential details in the current support log or tracking method. Once the ticketing system is implemented, requests may be recorded and monitored through the employee portal.

  3. Assess and assign. IT evaluates the issue, determines urgency and impact, and assigns an available technical staff member to assist.

  4. Troubleshoot and resolve. The assigned IT staff investigates the issue and performs necessary hardware or software troubleshooting. Issues that require purchase, additional access, or new software approval are referred to the relevant procedure.

  5. Confirm the result. IT tests the equipment or application and asks the employee to confirm that the reported problem has been resolved.

  6. Document and close. IT records the action taken, resolution date, assigned staff, and final status. Unresolved issues remain open for follow-up or escalation.

Important guidelines

Routine technical support may proceed without separate management signatures. Requests involving purchases, licensing, new software installations, or access permissions must obtain the approvals required by the applicable company procedure.

Implementation note

The Employee Portal Ticketing System is still pending. Until it becomes operational, employees should continue reporting concerns by phone or through Messenger, Viber, or Webex.
