Internal Request Portal

Reference guide for the desktop Internal Request Portal in YIMS. Covers self-service requests, supervisor authorization, and IT-assisted workflows for all user roles.

📋
Introduction

Portal Overview

The Internal Request Portal is a desktop application that allows Yakult employees to request printer cartridges from the IT department. The portal adapts its interface based on the user's role — normal employees see the standard request tabs, while supervisors and managers see additional authorization controls, and IT staff gain an extra tab to submit requests on behalf of others.

New Request

Submit a cartridge request by selecting model and quantity. Supports multiple models in a single submission.

My Requests

View, filter, and track the status of all requests you have previously submitted.

Authorization History

See every supervisor signature and approval action recorded against your requests.

Approver Area

For supervisors and managers — review and sign off on pending cartridge requests from their department.

Assisted Request

IT staff only — create a request on behalf of any employee in the system.

👤
Section 1

Roles & Access

The portal automatically detects your role from your employee profile. You do not need to select a role manually. The tabs and panels you see will differ based on the following access levels.

Normal Employee Access to New Request, My Requests, and Authorization History tabs. Cannot approve or assist others.
Higher-Up (Approver) All employee tabs plus the Approver Landing Panel and Approver Area. Can authorize pending requests from their own department. Can also submit their own request. Applies to: Supervisor, Manager, Coordinator, Account Coordinator, Asst. Coordinator, Jr. Asst. Manager, Asst. Manager, and related acting titles.
IT Staff All employee tabs plus the Assisted Request tab (amber header). Can create requests on behalf of any employee in the system.
ℹ️
Role is determined by your Position field Your access level is set by your employee position in the YIMS database and cannot be changed from within the portal. Contact your administrator if your access appears incorrect.

Section 2 — Normal Employees

Submitting a Request

Use the New Request tab to submit a cartridge request. Your company, branch, and department are auto-filled from your profile and cannot be changed — requests must be for your own department.

Step-by-Step: Single Model

  • Step 1: Open the New Request tab. Your destination details (Company, Branch, Department) are pre-filled automatically and are read-only.
  • Step 2: In the cartridge row, select a cartridge model from the dropdown. The dropdown shows the model name along with current available stock, e.g. HP 680 – Black (Available: 3). Only models with stock on hand are listed.
  • Step 3: Enter the Quantity you need. The field enforces a maximum equal to the available stock shown. A helper text "Max: X" is displayed below the field.
  • Step 4: Select the Cartridge Condition:
    • With Cartridge — you will return your used cartridges when picking up the new ones.
    • Without Cartridge — you have no cartridge to return. Enter the number of Good Empty and/or Damaged Empty cartridges if applicable.
  • Step 5: Choose the Fulfillment Method:
    • Pickup — you or a designated employee will collect the cartridges from the IT office. Select the receiving employee from the Received By dropdown.
    • Delivery — IT will deliver cartridges to your location. No receiver selection is required.
  • Step 6: Optionally enter any Remarks (up to 500 characters) for additional context.
  • Step 7: Click Submit Request. A confirmation dialog will appear. Click Confirm to send the request to IT for fulfillment.

Requesting Multiple Models at Once

You can add multiple cartridge models to a single submission. All models will be linked together as one session so IT can process them together.

  • Step 1: Fill in the first cartridge row as described above.
  • Step 2: Click Add Row to add another cartridge line. Repeat for each additional model you need.
  • Step 3: To remove a row, click the Remove (×) button on that row.
  • Step 4: Click Submit Request. All rows are submitted together as a single multi-model session.
💡
One submission = one session Fulfillment Method and Remarks apply to the entire submission. Each cartridge model gets its own request record, but IT sees them grouped together so all models can be processed in one exchange.

Rules & Restrictions

  • You can only request for your own department. The destination fields are locked to your employee profile.
  • Quantity cannot exceed the available stock shown in the dropdown at the time of submission.
  • Portal requests are intent-only — they do not immediately reduce stock. Inventory is only deducted when IT fulfills the request.
  • You cannot select a model that has zero available stock.

Section 3 — Normal Employees

My Requests Tab

The My Requests tab shows the full history of all cartridge requests you have submitted, with filtering and pagination.

Browsing Your Requests

  • Requests are displayed 10 per page. Use the pagination controls at the bottom to navigate.
  • Summary cards at the top show the total count broken down by status (Total, Under Review, On Hold, Submitted, Completed).
  • Click on any row to open the Request Detail dialog showing full information for that request: model, quantity, status, date, description, and fulfillment details.

Filtering & Searching

  • Use the Status dropdown to filter by a specific request status.
  • Use the Search field to search by model, request number, description, or other request details.
  • Use the Sort controls to reorder results by date, status, or other columns.

Actions

ActionDescription
ArchiveHides selected completed requests from the default view. Archived requests can still be found via search.
DeletePermanently removes a request. Only available for requests that have not yet been processed.
Export PDFGenerates a printable PDF summary of the selected request(s).
Mark SubmittedManually marks a request as Submitted when it has been handed off outside the system.

Section 4 — Normal Employees

Authorization History Tab

The Authorization History tab gives you a read-only view of every supervisor authorization recorded against your requests. Use this tab to confirm that your requests have been signed off.

  • Displays the approver's name, their position, the date and time of approval, and the approval decision.
  • Includes a preview of the approver's signature.
  • Requests that are still awaiting authorization will show a Pending status.
  • Rejected requests show a Rejected status with any notes left by the approver.
ℹ️
Authorization is required before fulfillment IT will not fulfill a request until a supervisor from your department has authorized it. If your request is taking longer than expected, check this tab to confirm it has been signed.

Section 5 — Higher-Ups

Approver Landing Panel

When you log in as a supervisor, manager, or coordinator, you will see the Approver Landing Panel — a three-card dashboard that provides a quick summary of pending authorization activity for your department.

CardWhat It Shows
Pending AuthorizationsThe number of requests from your department awaiting your signature. Badge updates in real time.

Section 6 — Higher-Ups

Authorizing Requests

Use the Approver Area to review and sign off on pending cartridge requests from employees in your department. Authorization is required before IT can fulfill any request.

How to Authorize a Request

  • Step 1: Click Authorize Cartridge Requests from the landing panel, or navigate to the Approver Area tab. The queue shows all pending requests from your department filtered by your DepartmentId.
  • Step 2: Click on a request row to view the full details: employee name, cartridge model, quantity requested, branch, and department.
  • Step 3: Review the pre-formatted authorization statement that summarizes the request in plain language.
  • Step 4: Provide your signature using one of the following methods:
    • Draw — use the signature canvas to draw your signature with your mouse or stylus.
    • Upload — click the upload button to import a saved signature image file.
  • Step 5: Click Approve to authorize the request, or Reject to decline it. You may optionally add notes before confirming either action.
  • Step 6: After approval, the request moves to Approved status and becomes visible to IT for fulfillment. The timestamp and your name are recorded.

Approval Queue

  • Displays Pending and Approved requests side by side for reference.
  • Each row shows the employee, model, quantity, and the date the request was submitted.
  • Signature previews are shown inline for requests you have already signed.
  • You can re-open any previously authorized request to review the signature record.
⚠️
Rejections notify the requester When you reject a request, the employee will be able to see the rejection and any notes you added in their Authorization History tab. Use clear and professional language in rejection notes.
🛠️
Section 7 — IT Staff

Assisted Request Tab

⚠ IT Staff Only — This tab is only visible to users with IT Staff access (Developer, Admin, IT Manager, Supervisor, Tech Support roles).

The Assisted Request tab allows IT staff to create a cartridge request on behalf of any employee in the system. This is used when an employee is unable to access the portal themselves or needs hands-on assistance. The tab header is displayed in amber to visually distinguish it from self-request tabs.

How to Submit an Assisted Request

  • Step 1: Navigate to the Assisted Request tab (amber header). This tab is only visible to IT staff accounts.
  • Step 2: Search for the target employee using the employee search or dropdown. Their Company, Branch, and Department will auto-fill from their employee profile — do not change these unless the employee profile is incorrect.
  • Step 3: If the employee is not listed (non-master employee), click Quick Add Employee to register them in the system with minimal details before proceeding.
  • Step 4: Select the cartridge model and quantity, condition, and fulfillment method exactly as you would for a self-request (see Section 2 above for details).
  • Step 5: For Pickup, select the Received By employee. This can be the requesting employee or a designated colleague.
  • Step 6: Click Submit Request. The request is created and tagged as PORTAL_ASSISTED in the system so IT staff can distinguish it from self-submitted requests.
