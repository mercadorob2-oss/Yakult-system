Request Portal (Web)

This guide is for supervisors, managers, and coordinators using the web-based Yakult Request Portal. It covers how to authorize pending requests from your department and how to submit your own personal cartridge requests.

🔒
Before You Begin

Logging In

The Request Portal runs in your web browser — no installation needed. You will need your Yakult employee credentials to access it.

  • Step 1: Open any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox) on your computer.
  • Step 2: Go to the Request Portal URL provided by your IT department.
  • Step 3: On the login page, enter your Employee ID and Password, then click Log In.
  • Step 4: Because your position is recognized as a higher-up, you will be taken directly to the Authorization Landing Page instead of the request form.
⚠️
Can't log in? If you don't have credentials or your login is not working, contact the IT department to have your account set up or reset. Do not share your password with anyone.
👑
Introduction

Your Role

As a higher-up, you have two responsibilities in the web Request Portal:

  1. Authorize cartridge requests submitted by employees in your department. IT cannot fulfill any request without your signature.
  2. Submit your own personal cartridge requests, the same way any regular employee would.
💡
Which positions qualify? Your role is detected automatically from your employee profile. The following positions are recognized as approvers:
Supervisor Manager Coordinator Account Coordinator Acting Account Coordinator Asst. Coordinator Lady Coordinator Jr. Asst. Manager Asst. Manager Acting Jr. Asst. Manager
🏠
Section 1

Authorization Landing Page

After logging in, you are taken to the Authorization Landing Page instead of the request form. This page has two options.

✅
Authorize Cartridge Requests

Review and sign pending requests from employees in your department. A badge shows how many are currently waiting for your action.

📄
Submit a Request

Submit your own personal cartridge request. Opens the same form available to all employees.

💡
Watch the pending badge The Authorize Cartridge Requests button shows a live count of requests awaiting your signature. Check it regularly — employees cannot get their cartridges until you sign off.
📜
Section 2

Approval Queue

Click Authorize Cartridge Requests to open the approval queue. This page lists all pending and previously authorized requests from your department — no requests from other departments are shown.

✏️
Section 3

Signing a Request

To authorize a request, open it from the queue and follow these steps.

  • Step 1: Click the request row. The full detail page opens with the employee's name, cartridge model, quantity, branch, department, and the pre-formatted authorization statement — read this carefully before signing.
  • Step 2: Provide your signature using one of these methods:
    • Draw — use the signature canvas on screen. Draw with your mouse or touch input.
    • Upload Image — click the upload button and select a saved signature image (PNG or JPG).
  • Step 3: Optionally add Notes — these are visible to the employee in their request history.
  • Step 4: Click Approve. The request status changes to Approved immediately. Your signature, name, and timestamp are recorded. IT can now proceed with fulfillment.
✅
After you approve The request moves from the Pending section to the Approved section of your queue. IT sees the approved status in the Cartridge Management Portal and can begin fulfillment.
❌
Section 4

Rejecting a Request

If a request should not be fulfilled, reject it from the same detail page.

  • Step 1: Open the request from the queue.
  • Step 2: Add a Note explaining the rejection reason. This is strongly recommended — the employee will see this in their request history.
  • Step 3: Click Reject. The request is declined, the employee is notified through their request history, and IT will not process it.
⚠️
Rejections are visible to the employee The employee can see your decision and notes in their request history. Keep your language clear and professional.
🚫
Approvals and rejections cannot be reversed through the portal Once confirmed, the action is immediately recorded. If you approved a request in error, contact IT before they fulfill it to have it placed on hold.
📄
Section 5

Submitting Your Own Request

Click Submit a Request from your landing page to submit your own personal cartridge request. The form works exactly the same as it does for regular employees.

Submitting a Single-Model Request

  • Step 1: The request form opens with your Company, Branch, and Department pre-filled and read-only.
  • Step 2: Select a cartridge model from the dropdown. Each option shows the model name and current available stock, e.g. HP 680 – Black (Available: 3). You may also type the model number directly.
  • Step 3: Enter the Quantity. The field shows a "Max: X" helper and will not accept values above the maximum.
  • Step 4: Select the Cartridge Condition — With Cartridge (you will return empties) or Without Cartridge (no empties to return).
  • Step 5: Choose your Fulfillment Method — Pickup (select a Received By person) or Delivery.
  • Step 6: Optionally add Remarks (up to 500 characters).
  • Step 7: Click Submit.
ℹ️
Your own requests also need authorization Your personal requests must be approved by another authorized approver from your department before IT can fulfill them. You cannot authorize your own requests.

Requesting Multiple Cartridge Models at Once

  • Step 1: Fill in the first cartridge row following the steps above.
  • Step 2: Click + Add Another Model to insert a new row. Each row has its own model, quantity, and condition fields.
  • Step 3: To remove a row, click the Remove (×) button. At least one row must remain.
  • Step 4: Fulfillment Method and Remarks apply to the whole submission. Set them once.
  • Step 5: Click Submit. All rows are sent as a single grouped session.
📋
Section 6

My Requests Page

The My Requests page shows only the requests you have personally submitted — not the requests you have authorized for others. Use it to track your own cartridge requests.

  • Each row shows: Request Number, Model, Quantity, Date Submitted, and Status.
  • Multi-model submissions appear as individual rows linked by session.
  • Click any row to view the full request detail.
  • Use the Status filter and Search field to narrow results.
ℹ️
Email notification on fulfillment When IT fulfills your request, you will receive an email with details of the cartridges issued, the fulfillment date, and any notes from the IT team.
