Request Portal (Web)

This guide is for regular employees using the web-based Yakult Request Portal. It covers how to submit a cartridge request from your browser, request multiple models at once, and track your requests through to fulfillment.

🔒
Before You Begin

Logging In

The Request Portal runs in your web browser — no installation needed. You will need your Yakult employee credentials to access it.

  • Step 1: Open any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox) on your computer.
  • Step 2: Go to the Request Portal URL provided by your IT department.
  • Step 3: On the login page, enter your Employee ID and Password, then click Log In.
  • Step 4: You will be taken directly to the request submission form.
⚠️
Can't log in? If you don't have credentials or your login is not working, contact the IT department to have your account set up or reset. Do not share your password with anyone.
🌐
Introduction

What Is This Portal?

The Request Portal is a browser-based tool that lets you submit cartridge requests to the IT department without needing to install any software. Log in with your Yakult employee credentials and the portal will automatically know who you are and where you work.

Submit a Request

Choose a cartridge model and quantity and submit your request in a few clicks. Works on any browser.

Multiple Models

Need more than one cartridge type? Add extra rows to your submission and send them all together.

Track Status

View all your requests and their current status — from awaiting authorization through to completed.

Email Notification

You will receive an email when IT has fulfilled your request, with details of what was issued.

ℹ️
Supervisor approval is required before fulfillment Every request must be authorized by a supervisor or manager from your department. Submitting a request does not immediately reserve stock — IT can only fulfill it after the supervisor signs off.
🏠
Section 1

Your Landing Page

After logging in, regular employees are taken directly to the Request Submission form. You will not see any authorization or approval controls — those are only shown to supervisors and managers.

💡
Landed on a different page? If you landed on an authorization or approval page, it means your employee position is recognized as a higher-up role. Refer to the Higher-Up guide instead, or click Submit a Request on that page to reach the submission form.
📄
Section 2

Request Form

Submitting a Request for One Cartridge Model

  • Step 1: The request form is shown immediately when you land on the portal. At the top you will see your Company, Branch, and Department — pre-filled from your employee profile and read-only. Confirm these are correct before continuing.
  • Step 2: Click the cartridge model dropdown. Each option shows the model name and current available stock, for example: HP 680 – Black (Available: 3). Only models with stock on hand appear. You may also type the model number directly — a typed value takes priority over the dropdown selection.
  • Step 3: Enter the Quantity you need. A "Max: X" helper text below the field shows the most you can request. The field will not accept a number higher than this.
  • Step 4: Select the Cartridge Condition:
    • With Cartridge — you will return your used (empty) cartridges when you receive the new ones.
    • Without Cartridge — you have no empties to return. If you do have some but they are damaged, enter the counts in the Good Empty and Damaged Empty fields. Their combined total cannot exceed your requested quantity.
  • Step 5: Choose your Fulfillment Method:
    • Pickup — you or a designee will collect from the IT office. Select the receiving employee in the Received By dropdown.
    • Delivery — IT will deliver to your location. No additional selection needed.
  • Step 6: Optionally enter any Remarks for additional context (up to 500 characters).
  • Step 7: Click Submit. A confirmation page will display your submitted request details and a reference number.
✅
Request submitted Your request has been sent. It will initially show as Awaiting Authorization in My Requests until your supervisor signs it. You will receive an email when it is fulfilled.

Requesting Multiple Cartridge Models at Once

You can include several cartridge models in a single submission. IT receives them as one grouped session and can process all models together.

  • Step 1: Fill in the first cartridge row following steps 2–4 above.
  • Step 2: Click + Add Another Model to insert a new row. Each row has its own model dropdown, quantity field, and condition selection.
  • Step 3: To remove a row you no longer need, click the Remove (×) button on that row. At least one row must remain.
  • Step 4: Set the Fulfillment Method and Remarks once — they apply to the entire submission.
  • Step 5: Click Submit. All rows are sent together as one session. Each model receives its own Request ID, but they are linked and processed as a group by IT.
💡
Quantity errors are shown per row If a row's quantity exceeds available stock, an error appears on that specific row. Reduce the quantity to the maximum shown and resubmit.

Rules & Restrictions

  • You can only request cartridges for your own department. The Company, Branch, and Department fields are locked to your profile.
  • Quantity per model cannot exceed the available stock shown in the dropdown at the time of submission.
  • Submitting a request does not immediately reduce stock. Stock is only adjusted when IT physically fulfills the request.
  • You cannot select a cartridge model with zero available stock — it will not appear in the dropdown.
📋
Section 3

My Requests Page

Use the My Requests page to track the status of all cartridge requests you have submitted through the web portal.

What You Can See

  • Each row shows: Request Number, Cartridge Model, Quantity, Date Submitted, and current Status.
  • Multi-model submissions appear as individual rows, linked by their session.
  • Click any row to view the full request details, including fulfillment notes and any remarks from IT.

Filtering

  • Use the Status dropdown to narrow the list to a specific stage, e.g. show only Completed requests.
  • Use the Search field to find a request by model name, request number, or other details.
ℹ️
You will receive an email when your request is fulfilled When IT processes your request, you will get an email confirmation listing the cartridges issued, the fulfillment date, and any notes from the IT team. You do not need to keep checking the portal.
📊
Reference

Stock & Availability

The Available: X count next to each cartridge model in the dropdown is calculated in real time:

  • Starts from the total physical stock on hand for that model.
  • Subtracts quantities already committed to other fulfilled or in-progress requests.
  • Portal requests (intent-only) do not reduce this count — only physically fulfilled requests do.
  • If the count reaches zero, the model will not appear in the dropdown.
⚠️
Stock can change between visits Availability is shown as of the moment you open the form. If another request is fulfilled for the same model before yours, IT will handle the allocation. If you receive a quantity error on submission, lower the quantity to the current maximum shown and try again.
