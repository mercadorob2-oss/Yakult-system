Internal Request Portal — IT Assisted Requests

This guide is for IT staff using the Assisted Request tab in the YIMS desktop application. It covers how to create a cartridge request on behalf of any employee in the system.

🔒
Before You Begin

Logging In

Before you can create assisted requests, you need to log in to the YIMS desktop application using your IT staff credentials.

  • Step 1: Open the YIMS application on your computer. You can find it on your desktop or in the Start menu.
  • Step 2: Enter your Employee ID and Password on the login screen, then click Log In.
  • Step 3: Once logged in, navigate to the Internal Request Portal from the main menu or sidebar. Because your account is recognized as IT Staff, you will see the additional Assisted Request tab with the amber header.
🛠️
Don't see the Assisted Request tab? Your account may not be assigned an IT staff role. Contact your administrator to confirm your role is correctly set to Developer, Admin, IT Manager, Supervisor, or Tech Support.
🛠️
Introduction

Your Role

IT staff have access to an extra tab in the Internal Request Portal: the Assisted Request tab. This tab allows you to submit a cartridge request on behalf of any employee — for example, when an employee cannot access the portal themselves, needs in-person help, or is a non-master employee not yet in the system.

Assisted requests go through the same workflow as employee self-requests: they require supervisor authorization from the employee's department before you can fulfill them through the Cartridge Management Portal.

🛠️
Who qualifies as IT Staff? The Assisted Request tab is visible to users with the following roles: Developer, Admin, IT Manager, Supervisor (IT), Tech Support. Access is enforced server-side regardless of what the UI shows.

Section 2

Assisted Request Tab

⚠  The Assisted Request tab has an amber-colored header to visually distinguish it from the standard New Request tab. Always double-check you are on the correct tab before submitting.

Step 1 — Finding the Employee

The first step is to identify who the request is for.

  • 1a. Use the Employee Search field or dropdown to find the target employee. You can search by name or employee ID.
  • 1b. Once you select an employee, their Company, Branch, and Department are automatically filled in from their employee profile. These fields are read-only — do not attempt to change them unless the employee's profile is incorrect in the system.

Step 2 (If Needed) — Quick Add Employee

If the employee you are assisting is not yet in the YIMS employee master list, use Quick Add Employee to register them before proceeding.

  • 2a. Click the Quick Add Employee button near the employee search.
  • 2b. Enter the minimum required details: employee name, department, and branch.
  • 2c. Save the record. The employee will now appear in the search dropdown and you can select them normally.
⚠️
Quick Add creates a minimal record Quick Add is intended for immediate assisted submissions only. Follow up with HR or your administrator to ensure the employee's full profile is completed in the system.

Step 3 — Filling in the Request

Once the employee is selected, fill in the cartridge details exactly as you would for a self-request.

  • 3a. In the cartridge row, select the cartridge model from the dropdown. The dropdown shows the model name and available stock, e.g. HP 680 – Black (Available: 3).
  • 3b. Enter the Quantity. The field enforces the maximum available stock. A helper text "Max: X" is shown below the field.
  • 3c. Select the Cartridge Condition:
    • With Cartridge — the employee will hand over their used empties at pickup or during delivery exchange.
    • Without Cartridge — no empties to return. Enter Good Empty and/or Damaged Empty counts if applicable. Their combined total cannot exceed the requested quantity.
  • 3d. To add more than one cartridge model, click Add Row. Each row has its own model, quantity, and condition. Click the Remove (×) button to delete a row you no longer need.
  • 3e. Select the Fulfillment Method:
    • Pickup — select the employee (or a designee) in the Received By dropdown who will collect from the IT office.
    • Delivery — IT will deliver to the employee's registered location. No receiver selection needed.
  • 3f. Optionally add Remarks (up to 500 characters) for any context or special instructions.

Step 4 — Submitting

  • 4a. Review all details before submitting. Confirm the correct employee is selected and the destination fields (Company, Branch, Department) are accurate.
  • 4b. Click Submit Request and confirm in the dialog that appears.
  • 4c. The request is created and tagged as PORTAL_ASSISTED in the system so it is distinguishable from employee self-requests in all views and reports.
✅
Request submitted successfully The assisted request enters the standard workflow. It will appear in the employee's request history (if they have access) and in the approval queue for their department's supervisor.
