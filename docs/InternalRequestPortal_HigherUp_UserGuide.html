Higher-Up

Internal Request Portal

This guide is for supervisors, managers, and coordinators using the Internal Request Portal in YIMS. It covers how to authorize pending requests from your department and how to submit your own personal cartridge requests.

🔒
Before You Begin

Logging In

Before you can authorize requests or submit your own, you need to log in to the YIMS desktop application using your Yakult employee credentials.

  • Step 1: Open the YIMS application on your computer. You can find it on your desktop or in the Start menu.
  • Step 2: Enter your Employee ID and Password on the login screen, then click Log In.
  • Step 3: Once logged in, navigate to the Internal Request Portal from the main menu or sidebar. Because your position is recognized as a higher-up, you will land directly on the Approver Landing Panel.
⚠️
Can't log in? If you don't have credentials or your login is not working, contact the IT department to have your account set up or reset.
👑
Introduction

Your Role in the Portal

As a higher-up, you have two responsibilities in the Internal Request Portal:

  1. Authorize cartridge requests submitted by employees in your department — IT cannot fulfill a request without your signature.
  2. Submit your own personal cartridge requests, the same way any employee would.
💡
Which positions qualify as Higher-Up? Your access is determined automatically from your employee position. The following titles are recognized as approvers:
Supervisor Manager Coordinator Account Coordinator Acting Account Coordinator Asst. Coordinator Lady Coordinator Jr. Asst. Manager Asst. Manager Acting Jr. Asst. Manager

Section 4

Signing a Request

To authorize a request, open it from the Approval Queue and follow these steps.

  • Step 1: Click the request row in the Approver Area. The full detail page opens showing the employee's name, cartridge model, quantity, branch, department, and the pre-formatted authorization statement summarizing the request.
  • Step 2: Read the authorization statement carefully. This is the formal record of your approval.
  • Step 3: Provide your signature using one of the following methods:
    • Draw — use the on-screen signature canvas. Draw your signature with your mouse or a stylus.
    • Upload Image — click the upload button to import a saved signature image (PNG or JPG).
  • Step 4: Optionally add Notes in the text box. Notes are visible to the requester in their Authorization History tab.
  • Step 5: Click Approve. The request status changes to Approved, your signature and timestamp are recorded, and IT is able to proceed with fulfillment.
✅
After approval The request disappears from the Pending section and moves to the Approved section of the queue. IT will see it in the Cartridge Management Portal and can begin fulfillment.
❌
Section 5

Rejecting a Request

If a request should not be fulfilled, you can reject it from the same detail page you use for approval.

  • Step 1: Open the request from the Approver Area.
  • Step 2: Add a Note explaining the reason for rejection. This is strongly recommended — the employee will see this note in their Authorization History tab.
  • Step 3: Click Reject. The request status changes to Rejected and the employee is notified through their request history.
⚠️
Rejections are visible to the employee The employee can see the rejection decision and your notes in their Authorization History tab. Use clear and professional language in rejection notes.
🚫
Approvals and rejections cannot be undone through the portal Once you confirm either action, the status is recorded immediately. If you approved a request in error before IT fulfills it, contact the IT department to place it on hold.
📄
Section 6

Submitting Your Own Request

You can submit your own personal cartridge request the same way any employee would. Click New Request from the tab bar or use the Submit a Request shortcut on your landing panel.

Submitting a Single-Model Request

  • Step 1: Open the New Request tab. Your Company, Branch, and Department are pre-filled and read-only.
  • Step 2: Select a cartridge model from the dropdown. Each option shows the model name and available stock, e.g. HP 680 – Black (Available: 3).
  • Step 3: Enter the Quantity you need. The field shows "Max: X" indicating the maximum allowed.
  • Step 4: Select the Cartridge Condition — With Cartridge (you have empties to return) or Without Cartridge (you do not).
  • Step 5: Choose your Fulfillment Method — Pickup (select a Received By person) or Delivery.
  • Step 6: Optionally add Remarks (up to 500 characters).
  • Step 7: Click Submit Request and confirm.
ℹ️
Your own requests also need authorization Even if you are an approver, your personal requests must be authorized by another approver from your department (or one above you) before IT can fulfill them. You cannot approve your own requests.

Requesting Multiple Cartridge Models at Once

  • Step 1: Fill in the first cartridge row as above.
  • Step 2: Click Add Row to insert another model line. Repeat for each additional model.
  • Step 3: To remove a row, click the Remove (×) button on that row.
  • Step 4: The Fulfillment Method and Remarks apply to the entire submission. Set them once.
  • Step 5: Click Submit Request and confirm. All rows are sent as a single grouped session.
📋
Section 7

My Requests Tab

The My Requests tab shows all cartridge requests that you personally have submitted (not the requests from your department's employees). Use it to track the status of your own requests.

  • Requests are shown 10 per page with pagination controls at the bottom.
  • Summary cards at the top break down your requests by status: Total, Under Review, On Hold, Submitted, Completed.
  • Click any row to open the full Request Detail dialog.
  • Use the Status dropdown and Search box to filter the list.

Available Actions

ActionWhat It Does
ArchiveHides the request from the default view. Still searchable.
DeletePermanently removes the request. Only available before IT processes it.
Export PDFGenerates a printable PDF summary of the selected request.
Mark SubmittedManually marks a request as Submitted for requests handled outside the system.
📄
Section 8

Authorization History Tab

The Authorization History tab shows the approval records for your own submitted requests — not the ones you have approved for others. Use it to check whether another approver has signed your personal requests.

  • Displays the approver's name, position, decision, date, and a signature preview.
  • Requests showing Pending have not yet been reviewed by another approver.
  • Requests showing Approved have been signed and are ready for IT fulfillment.
  • Requests showing Rejected were declined — check the Notes column for the reason.
🔹
