Internal Request Portal

This guide is for regular employees using the Internal Request Portal on the YIMS desktop application. It covers how to submit cartridge requests, track their progress, and read your authorization history.

🔒
Before You Begin

Logging In

Before you can submit or track any requests, you need to log in to the YIMS desktop application using your Yakult employee credentials.

  • Step 1: Open the YIMS application on your computer. You can find it on your desktop or in the Start menu.
  • Step 2: Enter your Employee ID and Password on the login screen, then click Log In.
  • Step 3: Once logged in, navigate to the Internal Request Portal from the main menu or sidebar.
⚠️
Can't log in? If you don't have credentials or your login is not working, contact the IT department to have your account set up or reset.
📋
Introduction

What Is This Portal?

The Internal Request Portal lets you request printer cartridges from the IT department directly through the YIMS desktop application. You fill in a form, your supervisor approves it, and IT prepares and delivers (or readies for pickup) your cartridges.

New Request

Submit a cartridge request by choosing a model and quantity. You can request multiple models in one go.

My Requests

View all requests you have submitted, check their current status, and export or archive them.

Authorization History

See whether your supervisor has signed off on your request, and view their signature and notes.

ℹ️
Supervisor approval is required Every request must be authorized by a supervisor or manager from your department before IT can fulfill it. Submitting a request does not immediately reserve stock — it is intent only until IT processes it.

Section 2

New Request Tab

Submitting a Request for One Cartridge Model

  • Step 1: Click the New Request tab. Your Company, Branch, and Department are shown at the top and are pre-filled automatically from your employee profile. These fields are read-only.
  • Step 2: In the cartridge row, click the model dropdown. Each option shows the cartridge name and how many are currently available, for example: HP 680 – Black (Available: 3). Only models with stock on hand are listed.
  • Step 3: Enter the Quantity you need. A helper text below the field shows the maximum you can request ("Max: X"). You cannot enter a number higher than this.
  • Step 4: Select the Cartridge Condition:
    • With Cartridge — you will hand over your used (empty) cartridges when collecting the new ones.
    • Without Cartridge — you have no empties to return. If you do have empties but they are damaged, enter the count in the Good Empty and Damaged Empty fields that appear. Their combined total cannot exceed your requested quantity.
  • Step 5: Choose your Fulfillment Method:
    • Pickup — you or a colleague will collect the cartridges from the IT office. Select the person who will pick them up in the Received By dropdown.
    • Delivery — IT will bring the cartridges to your location. No additional selection needed.
  • Step 6: Optionally type any Remarks in the text box (up to 500 characters). Use this for any special instructions or context you want IT to know.
  • Step 7: Click Submit Request. A confirmation dialog will appear — click Confirm to send the request.
✅
Request submitted After confirming, your request appears in the My Requests tab with a status of Awaiting Authorization. Your supervisor will be notified to review and sign it.

Requesting Multiple Cartridge Models at Once

You can include more than one cartridge model in a single submission. IT will receive all the models grouped together as one session, making it easier for them to process everything at once.

  • Step 1: Fill in the first cartridge row following steps 2–4 above.
  • Step 2: Click Add Row to add another cartridge line beneath. Repeat this for each additional model you need.
  • Step 3: To remove a row you no longer need, click the Remove (×) button on that row.
  • Step 4: Set the Fulfillment Method and Remarks once — these apply to the entire submission.
  • Step 5: Click Submit Request and confirm. All rows are sent together as one grouped session.
💡
Each model gets its own request number Even in a multi-model submission, each cartridge model is assigned its own Request ID. You will see them listed individually in My Requests, but IT sees them as a grouped session.

Rules & Restrictions

  • You can only request cartridges for your own department. The Company, Branch, and Department fields are locked to your employee profile and cannot be changed.
  • Quantity cannot exceed the available stock shown in the dropdown at the time of submission. The system will block submission if the number is too high.
  • Submitting a request does not immediately reduce stock. Inventory is only adjusted when IT physically fulfills the request.
  • You cannot select a cartridge model that shows zero available stock.

Section 3

My Requests Tab

The My Requests tab is your personal history of all cartridge requests you have submitted. It updates automatically as IT and your supervisor take action.

Reading the List

  • Requests are shown 10 per page. Use the pagination controls at the bottom to move between pages.
  • Summary cards at the top show the total count of your requests broken down by status: Total, Under Review, On Hold, Submitted, and Completed.
  • Click any row to open a Request Detail dialog showing the full information for that request — model, quantity, status, date submitted, fulfillment details, and any notes.

Filtering & Searching

  • Use the Status dropdown to filter the list to a specific status (e.g., show only Completed requests).
  • Use the Search box to find requests by model name, request number, or description.
  • Use the Sort controls to reorder results by date, status, or other columns.

Actions on Your Requests

Section 4

Authorization History Tab

The Authorization History tab shows you a read-only record of every supervisor authorization event tied to your requests. Use it to confirm your requests have been signed off and to check for any rejections.

What You Can See

  • The name and position of the approver who signed (or rejected) your request.
  • The date and time the authorization action was taken.
  • The decision: Approved or Rejected.
  • Any notes the approver left explaining their decision.
  • A preview of the approver's signature.

Status Indicators

StatusMeaning
PendingYour request has not yet been reviewed by a supervisor. It is still waiting in their queue.
ApprovedA supervisor has signed the request. IT can now proceed with fulfillment.
RejectedA supervisor declined the request. Check the notes column to understand why. You can submit a new request if appropriate.
⚠️
Request stuck on Awaiting Authorization? If your request has been sitting without approval for a while, check this tab to confirm it was received, then follow up with your supervisor directly. IT cannot fulfill the request until a supervisor signs off.
