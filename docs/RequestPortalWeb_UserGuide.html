Request Portal (Web)

Reference guide for the browser-based Yakult Request Portal. Covers self-service cartridge requests for all employees and the supervisor authorization workflow for higher-ups.

🌐
Introduction

Portal Overview

The Request Portal is a web-based application that allows Yakult employees to submit cartridge requests to the IT department from any browser — without needing to install software. The portal uses your employee login to identify who you are and automatically determines your role and destination details.

Self-Service Requests

Any employee can submit a cartridge request by selecting a model, quantity, and delivery preference.

Multi-Model Submissions

Add multiple cartridge models to a single submission. IT processes them as one grouped session.

Supervisor Authorization

Higher-ups have a dedicated landing page and approval queue to sign off on requests from their department.

Request Tracking

Track the status of your submitted requests from submission through to fulfillment.

💡
Requests are intent-only Submitting a request does not immediately reduce inventory. Stock is only deducted when IT physically fulfills the request from the Cartridge Management Portal. The availability shown in the dropdown reflects current net stock excluding other pending portal requests.
👤
Section 1

Roles & Landing Pages

After logging in, the portal automatically routes you to the correct landing page based on your employee position. You do not need to choose a role.

Normal Employee Directed straight to the Request Submission form. Can submit requests and view their request history.
Higher-Up (Approver) Directed to the Authorization Landing Page with two options: authorize pending requests from their department, or submit their own personal request. Applies to: Supervisor, Manager, Coordinator, Account Coordinator, Asst. Coordinator, Jr. Asst. Manager, Asst. Manager, and related acting titles.
ℹ️
Approvers can also submit requests If you are a supervisor or manager, you will land on the authorization page — but you can always choose Submit a Request to submit your own personal cartridge request using the same form as other employees.
📄
Section 2 — Normal Employees

Submitting a Request

Use the request form to submit a new cartridge request. Your Company, Branch, and Department are automatically filled in from your employee profile and are read-only — requests are always for your own department.

Step-by-Step: Single Cartridge Model

  • Step 1: Log in to the portal. Normal employees will land directly on the request form. If you are a higher-up, click Submit a Request from your landing page.
  • Step 2: Your Company, Branch, and Department are shown at the top of the form. These are read-only and pulled from your employee profile. Verify they are correct before proceeding.
  • Step 3: In the cartridge row, select your model from the dropdown. Each option shows the model name and current available stock:
    • Example: HP 680 – Black (Available: 3)
    • Only models with stock greater than zero appear in the list.
    • You may also type the model number directly — a typed model takes priority over the dropdown selection.
  • Step 4: Enter the Quantity you need. The field shows a helper text "Max: X" to indicate the maximum you can request. The field will not accept a value higher than the available stock.
  • Step 5: Select the Cartridge Condition:
    • With Cartridge — you will return your used cartridges when picking up the replacements.
    • Without Cartridge — you have no empties to return. If applicable, enter the number of Good Empty and/or Damaged Empty cartridges. The combined total cannot exceed the requested quantity.
  • Step 6: Choose your Fulfillment Method:
    • Pickup — you or a designee will collect from the IT office. Select the Received By employee from the dropdown.
    • Delivery — IT will deliver to your registered location. No receiver selection needed.
  • Step 7: Optionally add Remarks for any additional context (up to 500 characters).
  • Step 8: Click Submit. A confirmation page will be shown with your request details and a reference number.

Requesting Multiple Cartridge Models

You can request several different cartridge models in a single submission. All models will be linked as one session so IT can fulfill them together.

  • Step 1: Fill in the first cartridge row as above.
  • Step 2: Click + Add Another Model to insert a new row. Each row has its own model dropdown, quantity, and condition fields.
  • Step 3: To remove a row, click the Remove (×) button on that row. At least one row must remain.
  • Step 4: All rows share the same Fulfillment Method and Remarks. Set these once for the whole submission.
  • Step 5: Click Submit. All rows are submitted together. Each model gets its own request record, but they are all linked by a shared session ID so IT processes them as a group.
💡
Quantity validation per row Each row is validated independently. If any row exceeds available stock, you will see an error on that specific row before submission. Correct the quantity and resubmit.
📋
Section 3 — Normal Employees

Viewing My Requests

After submitting, you can track the status of all your requests from the My Requests page.

  • Each request shows: Request #, Cartridge Model, Quantity, Date Submitted, and current Status.
  • Multi-model submissions are shown as individual rows, but grouped by their session for easy reference.
  • Click any row to see the full request details including fulfillment notes and any remarks from IT.
  • Use the Status filter to narrow results to a specific stage (e.g., Awaiting Authorization, Approved, Completed).
ℹ️
Email notification on fulfillment When IT fulfills your request, you will receive an email notification confirming the cartridges issued, the fulfillment date, and any notes from the IT team.

Section 4 — Higher-Ups

Authorization Landing Page

When you log in as a supervisor, manager, or coordinator, you land on the Authorization Landing Page instead of the request form. This page gives you two quick-access options.

✅
Authorize Cartridge Requests

Review and sign pending requests from employees in your department. A badge shows how many are currently waiting.

📄
Submit a Request

Submit your own personal cartridge request. Opens the same request form available to all employees.

💡
Pending count badge The Authorize Cartridge Requests button shows a live count of requests currently awaiting your signature. Check this regularly to avoid delays for employees in your department.

Section 6 — Higher-Ups

Signing a Request

To authorize a request, open it from the Approval Queue and follow the steps below.

How to Authorize

  • Step 1: Click the request row in the queue. The full request detail page opens showing: employee name, cartridge model, quantity, branch, department, and the pre-formatted authorization statement.
  • Step 2: Read the authorization statement carefully. It summarizes the request in plain language for your records.
  • Step 3: Provide your signature using one of the following methods:
    • Draw — use the on-screen signature canvas to draw your signature with a mouse or touch input.
    • Upload Image — click the upload button to import a saved signature image (PNG or JPG).
  • Step 4: Optionally enter Notes to accompany your decision (recommended when rejecting).
  • Step 5: Click Approve to authorize, or Reject to decline the request.
    • Approve — the request status changes to Approved and IT can proceed with fulfillment. Your signature and timestamp are recorded.
    • Reject — the request is declined. The employee will see the rejection and your notes in their request history.
⚠️
Authorization is required for fulfillment IT will not process any request until it has been signed by an authorized approver from the requesting employee's department. Prompt authorization prevents delays.
🚫
Approvals cannot be undone through the portal Once you approve or reject a request, the action is recorded and the status changes immediately. If you approved a request in error, contact the IT department to place it on hold before fulfillment occurs.

Stock & Availability

The Available count shown next to each cartridge model in the dropdown is calculated as follows:

  • Starts from the total stock on hand for that model.
  • Subtracts quantities already committed to other non-portal requests (requests that have already been physically allocated).
  • Portal requests (like the one you are about to submit) are intent-only and do not reduce the available count until IT fulfills them.
  • A model will not appear in the dropdown if its effective available stock is zero or below.
⚠️
Availability can change between sessions The stock count is live at the time you open the form. If another user submits a request for the same model before yours is fulfilled, IT will handle the allocation on a first-fulfilled basis. If you see a quantity error on submission, reduce your quantity to the current maximum shown.

Request Statuses

StatusWhat it meansNext step
Awaiting Authorization Your request has been submitted and is waiting for a supervisor in your department to sign it. Wait for your supervisor to approve. Follow up if delayed.
Approved A supervisor has signed the request. IT will now proceed with fulfillment. No action needed. Wait for IT to fulfill.
Pending The request is with IT and is being actively processed. No action needed. Await fulfillment notification.
Under Review IT or a supervisor is reviewing the request before acting. Contact IT if this status persists unexpectedly.
On Hold The request has been temporarily suspended. Check with IT or your supervisor for the reason.
Completed IT has fulfilled the request. You will have received an email notification. Check your email for fulfillment details.
Rejected The request was declined by a supervisor. No cartridges will be issued. Review the rejection notes and resubmit if appropriate.
